ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Department of Biotechnology

An interdisciplinary academic environment connecting life science, biotechnology and modern scientific application.

DEPARTMENT PROFILE

A multidisciplinary bridge between biological knowledge and application.

Biotechnology brings together complementary biological and life-science disciplines to understand living systems and develop useful scientific applications.

The department's academic environment connects theoretical study with laboratory practice, scientific observation, analytical methods and evidence-based reasoning.

Students progressively encounter molecular, cellular, microbial, plant, biochemical and computational perspectives as part of their broader biotechnology education.

VISION & MISSION

Building scientific capability through education, practice and research.

The department seeks to provide a strong academic foundation in biotechnology while supporting scientific thinking, practical competence and responsible application.

01

Scientific Education

Develop strong foundations in biological sciences and the disciplines that support biotechnology.

02

Practical Competence

Strengthen laboratory observation, scientific measurement, analytical thinking and experimental practice.

03

Research Orientation

Encourage scientific inquiry and connect academic learning with biological research and emerging applications.

04

Responsible Application

Promote scientifically informed and responsible approaches to biotechnology and its applications.

ACADEMIC SCOPE

Biotechnology across multiple levels of life.

The interdisciplinary nature of biotechnology brings together scientific perspectives that range from molecular mechanisms to biological systems and data.

01

Molecular

DNA, RNA, genes and molecular mechanisms.

02

Cellular

Cells, tissues and biological organization.

03

Microbial

Microorganisms, fungi and microbial systems.

04

Plant

Plant systems and biotechnology applications.

05

Biochemical

Biomolecules, reactions and analytical science.

06

Computational

Biological data and computational analysis.

ACADEMIC IDENTITY

Understanding living systems from molecule to application.

The Department's interdisciplinary character connects foundational science with specialized biotechnology study, practical laboratory learning and scientific inquiry.

This structure gives biotechnology a broad academic identity while maintaining a clear connection between biological understanding and practical application.
