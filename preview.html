ACADEMIC PATHWAY

From biological foundations to modern biotechnology

The academic structure connects molecules, cells, genes, microorganisms, plants and biotechnology applications through complementary theoretical and practical learning.

01

Biological foundations

Cells, tissues, physiology and the organization of living systems.

02

Molecular and genetic sciences

DNA, RNA, proteins, inheritance, chromosomes and molecular mechanisms.

03

Microbial and plant systems

Microbiology, mycology, phycology, plant biotechnology and biofertilizers.

04

Applied biotechnology

Engineering, nanotechnology, analytical science, antibiotics and bioinformatics.
